## How We Build

Our engineering philosophy is straightforward:

**Correctness before automation.**  
Important calculations and decisions need verifiable foundations.

**Privacy by design.**  
Sensitive information deserves clear handling boundaries and appropriate protection.

**Evidence over assumptions.**  
Software should communicate uncertainty and limitations rather than invent answers.

**Usability without unnecessary complexity.**  
Sophisticated engineering should produce simpler experiences for users.

**Validation before release.**  
Testing, security review, and operational reliability are essential parts of building dependable products.

## Our Direction

Our work prioritizes products that deliver measurable utility rather than unnecessary features or complexity.
